  muted text and outlines. Keep raw palette colors for visualization blocks.
- Give resting buttons visible outlines, with stable widths across hover,
  press and focus. Separate toolbar controls from scan/view controls to keep
  them readable at laptop width.
- Increase treemap header and file-label sizes. Protect label backgrounds and
  retain full-strength size text. Correct colored list/duplicate/age text too.
- Stream directory entries and publish the active ancestor chain immediately
  after the first file, then every 250 ms inside unfinished directories.
  Bound previews to 16,384 nodes, preserve totals with non-actionable aggregate
  tiles, keep one queued preview, and preserve the camera during updates.

## Visual inspection

All 41 final PNGs were opened and inspected by the agent: both welcome modes,
dark and light maps, depth/age/extension palettes, list, largest files, extension
blocks, duplicate fixture, About, theme editor, hover/press/focus, tooltip,
extension filtering, and five live scan stages.

The first image review caught overlapping laptop toolbar controls and raw
palette text with poor contrast in list views. Both were corrected before the
final run. Final controls stay within the toolbar; the theme editor and About
fit the reviewed sizes; labels and tooltips remain readable in both modes.
Button-state captures also assert actual egui hover/pressed/focus state.

Small treemap tiles still truncate or clip detail to their bounds. During pause
the action changes to Resume, but the status text still says Scanning and the
spinner remains animated. The stress run averaged 18.156 ms with a 347.486 ms
maximum frame, so its passing result establishes invariants and resource bounds,
not uniformly smooth frames. Large final-tree duplicate preparation is an
existing additional performance area to investigate.

Raw screenshots, CSVs, logs and the machine-readable review receipt remain in
the ignored `test-results/20261001-contrast-live-final/` folder. See
[GAUNTLET.md](GAUNTLET.md) for subsequent runs.
