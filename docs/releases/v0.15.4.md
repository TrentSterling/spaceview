Two final UI fixes:

- Treemap and file-type labels use measured glyph widths and ellipses. Rows that
  cannot fit vertically are hidden, and size values are shown only when complete.
  Directory names reserve the actual width of their size/count text. Unicode
  filenames are handled without byte slicing.
- Paused scans say **Paused**, show a static pause indicator, and hide the scan
  rate until resumed. This applies before and after the first preview arrives.

Validation: 46 tests passed, native scan pause/resume/cancel passed, and the
500,000-file stress gauntlet passed. Dense treemaps and paused/resumed captures
were visually inspected at the relevant window sizes.
